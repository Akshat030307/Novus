# Shot list

About 1:42 in total, which fits inside one play of the music (1:43).

**Cards** (in `cards/`) are full-frame stills: drop each one on the timeline
between clips for the time given. Two kinds: **stat cards** (one big figure,
2 s) and **screenshot cards** (a headline beside a crop of the game, 3 s).
A 0.2 s fade either side is plenty.

**The one claim every scene has to prove:** Novus grades your reasoning, never
your luck. Scenes 02–08 show that from the player's side. Scenes 09–10 show what
a school or company gets out of it.

Each scene lists what to load, what to press, which moments deserve a close-up,
which caption to use, and the reference frame in `screenshots/` showing what the
camera should see. Take numbers refer to the recording order in the README.

**Where things are:** the Bank is north-west of the plaza, the Exchange
north-east, Risk & Compliance far east, and the Academy south. You start on the
plaza, just below the fountain.

## 05 · News, or noise? — 0:32–0:44

Two stocks with almost the same move and completely different causes.

- **Load:** B → Continue. It's 11:14 on day 2, an hour after the headline
  *"Government clears ₹40,000 crore of highway and port projects"*.
- **Do:**
  1. Press **→** until the **Market** tab is open. Sethu Infra is already
     selected: up about 5%.
  2. Click **▸ Why did it move today?** and scroll the panel one notch. The bar
     is mostly **amber (news)**, and the line reads *"Today was driven by more
     than noise."*
  3. Scroll down the stock table and click **Tarang Payments**, which is up
     about 4%.
  4. Scroll up to its header, then one notch down to the breakdown. The bar is
     almost all **grey (noise)**, news is **₹0**, and the line reads *"Most of
     today was noise — movement that means nothing and will not repeat."*
- The clock keeps running, so the exact figures drift slightly between takes.
  The captions don't depend on them.
- **Close-up (take 2c, 150%):** the breakdown, for each stock.
- **Captions:** `05a-same-move.png` on Sethu, `05b-news-or-noise.png` on Tarang.

## 10 · What to teach next week — 1:26–1:36

The only scene from the teacher's side. It uses the **sample class**: twelve
invented students, clearly labelled, built into the page. There's nothing to
load and no need to sign in.

- **Load:** open `http://localhost:5173/teach/sample` in the recording profile.
  Leave **Show cursor** on.
- **Do:** the whole dashboard fits one 1920×1080 screen, so there's no scrolling.
  1. Hold for a beat on the full view. The orange *Sample data — not real
     students* badge next to the class name stays in shot. Keep it there: it's
     honest, and it answers "are these real kids?" before anyone asks.
  2. Move the cursor slowly over the **Habits** heatmap and rest on Farhan
     Qureshi's *Trading the noise* cell (the brightest one, 4). A tooltip
     reads *4 times*.
  3. Click Farhan's name in the heatmap. Every chart dims to him: his bar in
     *Decisions*, his dot in the scatter, his own attempts per day. His
     numbers replace the four figures at the top.
  4. Press **Esc**, then click the red **5** on *The credit desk* in *Results
     by assignment*. The page switches to the **Students** tab, filtered to
     the five who haven't passed it yet.
- **Result, every time:** the same four lessons in the same order. The sample
  is fixed data, and only the due dates move so they always read as this week.
- **Close-up:** not needed. The page uses normal-size text, so it reads at 100%.
